Petal & Bloom began with a simple belief: flower delivery should be as delightful and effortless as receiving a bouquet in person.

We bypass middlemen and source directly from ethical growers who harvest at peak bud. Every stem is inspected, conditioned, and hand-tied by seasoned floral designers in our studio before reaching your hands.
